About Timuquana Country Club

Timuquana Country Club is one of Jacksonville's oldest and most prestigious private clubs, established in 1912 on the south bank of the St. Johns River in the Ortega neighborhood. The club's Spanish-style clubhouse and manicured grounds have been hosting member celebrations for more than a century, and the formal ballroom, river views, and classic country club aesthetic make it a consistently popular wedding venue for Jacksonville families with club connections.

As a private club, Timuquana Country Club events are typically available to members and their families. The club's full-service event team handles catering and event coordination in-house, and the formal ballroom is well-suited for traditional wedding receptions with a seated dinner and dancing.

Why Couples Choose Timuquana Country Club

  • Historic Jacksonville prestige: Timuquana is one of Jacksonville's oldest private clubs — there's an established elegance to the setting that newer venues haven't had time to develop.
  • St. Johns River setting: The club's position on the St. Johns River provides natural water views for outdoor ceremonies and cocktail hours.
  • Full-service club catering: The club handles catering and event coordination in-house, simplifying vendor management significantly.
  • Classic country club aesthetic: For couples who want a traditional, formal wedding setting rather than a rustic barn or modern hotel ballroom, the country club environment delivers exactly that.

How a Typical Wedding Day Flows Here

A Timuquana Country Club wedding typically begins with an outdoor ceremony on the club's grounds or terrace, with the St. Johns River visible in the background. Guests then move inside for cocktail hour before the formal ballroom reception.

From an entertainment standpoint, the formal ballroom is a well-proportioned reception space that accommodates a full DJ and dance floor setup comfortably. The outdoor ceremony requires a wireless microphone system. We coordinate load-in and setup logistics with the club's event staff in advance of your wedding day.
