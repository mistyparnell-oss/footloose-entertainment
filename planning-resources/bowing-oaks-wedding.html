About Bowing Oaks

Bowing Oaks is a private estate wedding venue in Jacksonville, FL — named for its defining feature: a dramatic canopy of ancient live oaks whose branches bow and arch overhead, creating a natural cathedral effect that serves as one of the most photographed ceremony settings in the Jacksonville area. The estate grounds combine manicured outdoor spaces with an indoor reception facility, giving couples flexibility across different weather conditions and event configurations.

Footloose Entertainment is on Bowing Oaks' preferred vendor list and provides wedding entertainment there regularly. That means we already know the property — the load-in process, power access at the ceremony oak grove, and how the indoor reception hall is configured — before we arrive on your wedding day.

Why Couples Choose Bowing Oaks

  • The oak canopy ceremony setting: The arching live oaks create a natural cathedral overhead that photographers consistently describe as one of the most beautiful ceremony backdrops in the Jacksonville area.
  • Estate privacy: As a private estate venue, Bowing Oaks offers exclusivity that hotel and museum venues can't match — your wedding is the only event on the property.
  • Indoor-outdoor flexibility: The combination of outdoor ceremony grounds and an indoor reception hall means couples get the outdoor ceremony they want with weather protection for dinner and dancing.
  • Specialized wedding experience: Bowing Oaks focuses exclusively on weddings and has event coordination expertise that shows in the day-of logistics.

How a Typical Wedding Day Flows Here

A Bowing Oaks wedding typically begins with an outdoor ceremony under the oak canopy — guests are seated on the estate lawn, the couple stands beneath the arching oaks, and the ceremony is framed entirely by the natural environment. After the ceremony, guests move to the estate patio for cocktail hour while the indoor reception hall is finalized.

The outdoor ceremony requires a wireless microphone and speaker system positioned to carry sound through the open-air oak grove. The indoor reception follows with a full DJ and dance floor setup. Because Footloose Entertainment is on Bowing Oaks' preferred vendor list, we already know the load-in sequence and how both spaces are configured.
