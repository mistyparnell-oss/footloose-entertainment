About Marywood Retreat & Conference Center

Marywood Retreat & Conference Center sits on 100 acres of wooded riverfront land at 235 Marywood Drive in St. Johns, Florida — in the Fruit Cove area of St. Johns County, about 20 minutes south of downtown Jacksonville. The property is operated by the Diocese of St. Augustine and has been a place of celebration and gathering for generations of Northeast Florida families.

What sets Marywood apart from other waterfront venues in the area is the combination of features that are genuinely hard to find together: a private dock and river views for ceremony and photos, 59 hotel-style villa rooms so out-of-town guests can stay on property for the weekend, Catholic-blessed ceremony grounds for couples who want a consecrated outdoor setting, and an in-house catering team that handles food, bar, and event coordination under one roof. For couples planning a destination-style wedding without leaving Northeast Florida, Marywood checks most of the boxes.

Why Couples Choose Marywood

  • Riverfront setting without the downtown price: Marywood offers genuine St. Johns River views and dock access in a setting that feels secluded and serene — without the cost of a downtown Jacksonville waterfront venue.
  • On-site accommodations for the whole guest list: With 59 villa rooms on property, Marywood functions as a weekend retreat as much as a wedding venue. Guests stay, eat, and celebrate together without anyone driving back to a hotel across town.
  • Catholic-blessed ceremony grounds: Marywood is a Catholic retreat center and its outdoor ceremony area is on consecrated land — one of the few venues in Northeast Florida where couples can have an outdoor ceremony on Catholic-blessed grounds outside of a church building.
  • All-inclusive event coordination: In-house catering, bar service, tables, chairs, linens, china, glassware, and a venue coordinator are part of the package — which simplifies the vendor list considerably compared to venues that require outside caterers.
  • Grand staircase and dock for photos: Marywood has a grand staircase and private dock that create natural photo moments that many couples specifically choose the property for.

How a Typical Wedding Day Flows Here

A Marywood wedding typically begins with the ceremony on the outdoor grounds, where guests are seated facing the ceremony area beneath the live oaks. The property’s natural landscape — moss, mature trees, river glimpses — gives outdoor ceremonies here a lush, elevated feel that doesn’t require much additional decoration to look beautiful in photos.

After the ceremony, the wedding party usually takes advantage of the dock and staircase for portraits while guests move inside the River Room for cocktail hour. Dinner and dancing follow in the River Room, with the back patio open for guests who want to step outside for some air and river views during the reception. Marywood’s event coordinator handles the flow with the in-house team, which means the evening stays on track without requiring couples to manage logistics themselves.

Because the property has accommodations, the timeline often extends a bit later than at venues where everyone drives home — guests who are staying on property tend to linger and celebrate longer, which we plan for when building the entertainment timeline.

Planning Your Entertainment

Footloose Entertainment is a preferred vendor at Marywood Retreat, which means we have an established working relationship with their event coordinator and a clear picture of how the property’s spaces operate during a wedding. As your DJ at Marywood, we coordinate load-in, power access, and setup logistics directly with Marywood’s team well in advance — so none of those details are getting figured out on your wedding day.

The main entertainment planning consideration at Marywood is that the outdoor ceremony and indoor reception are two separate setups. The outdoor ceremony needs a wireless microphone system and speakers positioned to carry sound across an open-air setting. The River Room reception is a full DJ setup — dance floor speakers, subwoofer, uplighting, DJ booth. We account for both phases and the transition between them before your wedding day.

For uplighting, Marywood’s River Room is a good candidate — the space has architectural interest that uplighting enhances, and the evening river views from the patio create a nice backdrop when the lighting inside complements rather than competes with the natural setting outside.

What we confirm before every Marywood wedding: outdoor ceremony speaker placement and microphone setup, power access for the River Room reception, indoor DJ booth position, dance floor configuration, and timeline coordination with Marywood’s event coordinator.

If you’re adding a wedding photo booth, the River Room has space for a photo booth. We plan placement during pre-event coordination so the booth is positioned where guests naturally flow during cocktail hour and after dinner — not tucked into a corner where it gets passed over.
