About Old Spanish Quarter

Old Spanish Quarter is a historic wedding and event venue on Jacksonville’s Westside — a beautifully preserved property dating back to the 1800s that offers a setting unlike most venues in the Jacksonville metro area. The grounds combine lush garden spaces, classic Southern architecture, charming walkways, and intimate indoor areas in a way that photographs well without feeling staged or over-designed.

The venue is located at 13535 W Beaver St near NAS Jacksonville — a quieter, more private setting than Southside or Downtown venues, which suits couples who want an exclusive, personal atmosphere rather than a hotel ballroom experience. Footloose Entertainment is on Old Spanish Quarter’s preferred vendor list and provides wedding entertainment there regularly.

Why Couples Choose Old Spanish Quarter

  • Genuine historic character: The property’s 1800s origins give it an authenticity that purpose-built modern venues can’t replicate — the architectural details, garden patina, and overall feel are the real thing.
  • Private Westside location: The quiet setting near NAS Jacksonville means no competing events, no shared venue noise, and a private experience for your guests from arrival to send-off.
  • Indoor and outdoor flexibility: The combination of outdoor garden ceremony space and indoor reception areas gives couples options without requiring transportation between separate venues.
  • Photogenic at every turn: Couples and photographers consistently note how many distinct photo settings Old Spanish Quarter offers — gardens, walkways, architectural elements, and natural light from multiple angles.
  • Hands-on team: Old Spanish Quarter’s team operates the venue with a detail-oriented approach that shows in day-of execution.

How a Typical Wedding Day Flows Here

A typical Old Spanish Quarter wedding flows from an outdoor garden ceremony — guests seated beneath the property’s trees and garden surroundings — into the courtyard for cocktail hour, then to the indoor reception area for dinner and dancing. The property’s layout keeps all three phases on the same grounds, so guests don’t need to travel and the day feels cohesive rather than segmented.

The garden ceremony setting is intimate and naturally framed, working well for both larger ceremonies and smaller, more personal events. After the ceremony, the courtyard and walkways naturally serve as cocktail hour space while the indoor reception area is finalized for dinner service.
