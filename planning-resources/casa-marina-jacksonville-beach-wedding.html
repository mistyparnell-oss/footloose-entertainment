Casa Marina Hotel Wedding Guide

About Casa Marina Hotel

Casa Marina Hotel opened in 1925 as one of the first hotels in Jacksonville Beach, and it remains one of the few historic buildings in the area that has survived the resort town's evolution over the past century. The hotel sits one block from the Atlantic Ocean — close enough to hear the surf, but with the ballroom infrastructure and architectural character that an actual beach ceremony location typically can't provide.

Footloose Entertainment is on Casa Marina's preferred vendor list and provides wedding entertainment there regularly. We know the hotel's load-in process, power access in the ballroom, and the coordination rhythm with their event staff.

Why Couples Choose Casa Marina Hotel

  • Historic beach destination: Casa Marina is one of the only historic venues at the beach in the Jacksonville area — couples who want a beach destination wedding with real architectural character find a genuine option here.
  • Proximity to the ocean: The hotel is one block from the Atlantic — beach photos, beach ceremony add-ons, and a beachside weekend for out-of-town guests are all genuinely accessible.
  • Full hotel package: On-site catering, hotel guest rooms, and experienced event staff make Casa Marina a manageable all-in-one wedding venue at the beach.
  • Beach town atmosphere: Jacksonville Beach's walkability, boardwalk, and coastal atmosphere give the whole wedding weekend a distinct sense of place.

How a Typical Wedding Day Flows Here

A Casa Marina wedding typically centers on the Grand Ballroom for the reception, with couples often incorporating outdoor beach photos between the ceremony and reception. Some couples host outdoor ceremonies on the hotel's courtyard or nearby outdoor space, transitioning inside for dinner and dancing.

From an entertainment standpoint, the Grand Ballroom is the primary entertainment space — a full DJ and dance floor setup in a well-proportioned room with vintage hotel character. If an outdoor ceremony is part of the plan, a wireless microphone and speaker system handles that phase. Footloose Entertainment coordinates both setups with hotel event staff in advance.

Music & Atmosphere

Casa Marina Hotel brings a coastal, laid-back-elegant feel to Jacksonville Beach weddings — the beachside setting and the Grand Ballroom's classic bones give couples flexibility to run the evening anywhere from sophisticated to relaxed. The music program usually follows the couple's lead, and the venue's beach proximity means the vibe can tilt more festive than a formal country club would allow.

Ceremony music at Casa Marina often happens outdoors — on the courtyard, the hotel grounds, or even on the nearby beach for smaller ceremonies — which calls for a portable PA setup. Cocktail hour in a pre-function space transitions smoothly into the Grand Ballroom reception, which is where the main DJ setup lives.

Key acoustic and logistics notes:

  • The Grand Ballroom is a well-proportioned hotel ballroom — good for sound coverage with a standard DJ rig, without the hard-reflection issues you get in spaces with very high ceilings or bare concrete walls.
  • Outdoor ceremony setups at Casa Marina depend on which outdoor space you're using. The hotel's event team can confirm power access; a battery-powered system is a reliable backup for courtyard ceremonies.
  • The beach proximity is a genuine perk — guests often step outside between dinner and dancing, which naturally manages energy levels in the room. Plan for a re-entry moment when you want the dance floor to peak.

From our experience: Jacksonville Beach weddings at Casa Marina tend to run warmer and more relaxed than inland venues. Couples who lean into that — choosing a music program that matches the coastal setting — have some of the best dance floors we see all year.
