About Omni Amelia Island

Omni Amelia Island is a full-scale resort destination on the southern end of Amelia Island — one of the largest resort properties in Northeast Florida, with 3.5 miles of Atlantic beachfront, multiple ballrooms, championship golf, a full spa, and a comprehensive event infrastructure that handles everything from intimate weddings to large-scale corporate conferences. The resort underwent a significant renovation in recent years, bringing updated event spaces and guest rooms to an already well-established property.

Footloose Entertainment is on Omni Amelia Island's preferred vendor list and provides entertainment for weddings and corporate events at the resort. We work within the resort's established event management protocols and coordinate directly with their team in advance of your event day.

Music & Atmosphere

Omni Amelia Island is a full-scale oceanfront resort — the Atlantic Ocean as a ceremony backdrop, multiple ballrooms for large receptions, and a resort scale that makes it one of the most logistically capable venues in Northeast Florida. The music program at Omni Amelia Island has room to be as ambitious as the couple wants.

Outdoor ceremonies on the oceanfront event lawn are a signature moment — the ocean behind the couple, the resort grounds framing the setting, and a sound setup that has to work against open-air acoustics and ocean breeze. After the ceremony, guests typically move inside for cocktail hour and then the main ballroom reception, where the DJ setup is fully established and the sound environment is controlled.

Key logistics and acoustic notes:

  • Oceanfront ceremony audio requires a robust outdoor setup — the open-air environment and sea breeze mean sound dissipates faster than indoors. Plan for more speaker output than you'd need for an equivalently sized indoor ceremony.
  • The resort ballrooms are large, well-equipped event spaces. Power access is standard and load-in is coordinated through the resort's event team, which is experienced with entertainment vendors at this scale.
  • Guest accommodation on-site changes the evening's dynamics the same way any full-service resort wedding does — people stay later and engage more when they're not watching the clock for a drive home.
  • The Fernandina Beach / Amelia Island setting attracts guests who've made a destination of the weekend. Energy in the room tends to be high — match the music program to that.

From our experience: Omni Amelia Island receptions run big — large guest counts, high energy, and couples who've invested in a full destination-feel weekend. The dance floors reflect that. Come prepared to sustain it.
