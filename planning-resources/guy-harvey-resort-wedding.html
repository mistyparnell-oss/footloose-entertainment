About Guy Harvey Resort

Guy Harvey Resort is an oceanfront resort on A1A in St. Augustine Beach, FL — one of the few full-service venues in Northeast Florida that offers ballroom receptions, beach ceremonies, and on-site guest accommodations under one roof. The resort sits directly on A1A Beach Boulevard, giving it direct beach access for ceremonies and an oceanside atmosphere throughout the entire property.

For couples and event planners looking for a beachside St. Augustine venue that handles both wedding receptions and corporate events seriously, Guy Harvey Resort offers more event infrastructure than a typical boutique beach hotel while staying accessible in a way that larger resort properties sometimes don’t. Footloose Entertainment is on the resort’s preferred vendor list and provides entertainment for both weddings and corporate events there.

The Nautilus Ballroom at 2,800 square feet is the resort’s primary event space. The Fairwinds Room is a natural choice for rehearsal dinners, smaller receptions, or cocktail hours before the main ballroom event. Total banquet space across the property is approximately 3,000 square feet.

Beach ceremonies require a permit for events with 15 or more attendees. The resort’s event team can walk you through the current permit process during your site visit.

Why Couples Choose Guy Harvey Resort

  • Beach ceremony access: The ability to have a ceremony directly on the Atlantic Ocean is the resort’s signature offering — few Northeast Florida venues have genuine beach access with resort-level reception infrastructure behind it.
  • Everything in one place: Beach ceremony, ballroom reception, pool deck cocktail hour, and on-site hotel rooms for guest blocks — no transportation coordination between ceremony, reception, and hotel.
  • Oceanside atmosphere throughout: Even events held entirely inside the Nautilus Ballroom benefit from the resort’s beachside setting — guests arrive at a beach resort, not a suburban event center.
  • Guest room blocks: Out-of-town guests can stay on-site, which simplifies end-of-night logistics and often allows the celebration to run longer and more fully.
  • Accessible pricing in the St. Augustine market: Compared to some historic downtown St. Augustine venues, Guy Harvey Resort offers competitive pricing with the added value of beach access and on-site accommodations.

How a Typical Wedding Day Flows Here

A typical Guy Harvey Resort wedding runs in one of two configurations. The most popular: beach ceremony at sunset followed by cocktail hour on the pool deck or patio terrace, then dinner and dancing in the Nautilus Ballroom. The other common option skips the beach ceremony in favor of an indoor ceremony in the Nautilus Ballroom, followed immediately by a cocktail-style transition to reception.

For beach ceremonies, timing is everything — the resort’s event team coordinates the A1A beach access and permit requirements. We recommend the DJ arrive for Nautilus Ballroom load-in while the beach ceremony is underway, so the ballroom is fully set and ready when guests transition indoors after cocktail hour.

Corporate & Company Party Entertainment

Guy Harvey Resort’s Nautilus Ballroom and outdoor pool deck make it a capable corporate event venue for Jacksonville and St. Augustine–area companies. The 2,800-square-foot Nautilus Ballroom handles seated dinners, awards events, and general sessions for up to 150. The pool deck and outdoor beach lounge extend that into a more casual setting — cocktail receptions, team events, and informal company gatherings work especially well in the resort’s outdoor spaces.

Footloose Entertainment is on the resort’s preferred vendor list and provides DJ, music, and entertainment services for corporate events there. For company parties and holiday events at Guy Harvey, we typically focus on background music during cocktails and dinner, then transition to a more active dance floor set as the evening progresses — a common format at beachside resort corporate events where the atmosphere shifts naturally over the course of the night.

For corporate events at Guy Harvey Resort: the pool deck is a strong option for outdoor corporate cocktail hours and casual company events. The Nautilus Ballroom handles more formal seated dinners and presentations. We pre-coordinate AV needs, music programming, and day-of load-in timing with the resort’s event team. Corporate DJ packages start at $995.
