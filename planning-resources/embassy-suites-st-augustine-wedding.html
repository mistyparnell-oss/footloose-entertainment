About Embassy Suites St. Augustine

Embassy Suites St. Augustine is a full-service hotel offering one of the larger ballroom and event space footprints in the St. Augustine market. The hotel's all-suite accommodations, on-site catering, and experienced event team make it a practical choice for weddings and corporate events where out-of-town guests are a significant part of the picture and where vendor logistics need to be simplified.

Footloose Entertainment is on Embassy Suites St. Augustine's preferred vendor list and provides entertainment for both weddings and corporate events at the hotel. We work within the hotel's established vendor protocols and coordinate directly with their event management team — so the working relationship and logistics are already established before your event day.

Why Couples Choose Embassy Suites St. Augustine

  • All-suite accommodations for out-of-town guests: Every room in the hotel is a suite — out-of-town guests have genuinely comfortable accommodations right at the venue.
  • Spacious ballroom: The Grand Ballroom is one of the larger event spaces in St. Augustine proper, accommodating guest lists that many of the city's smaller historic venues cannot.
  • Full hotel infrastructure: On-site catering, bar service, AV capabilities, and experienced event staff simplify vendor coordination significantly.
  • Works for weddings and corporate events equally well: The hotel's ballroom environment and event infrastructure serve both wedding receptions and corporate gatherings.

How a Typical Wedding Day Flows Here

An Embassy Suites St. Augustine wedding typically begins with an outdoor ceremony on the hotel's terrace or grounds, followed by a cocktail hour in a pre-function area, then dinner and dancing in the Grand Ballroom.

From an entertainment standpoint, the Grand Ballroom is a well-equipped reception space for a full DJ and dance floor setup. The outdoor ceremony requires a wireless microphone system. Footloose Entertainment is on the hotel's preferred vendor list and coordinates both phases with the event team before your wedding day.

Music & Atmosphere

Embassy Suites St. Augustine occupies a uniquely convenient position for guests — the on-site accommodations mean most of your wedding party and out-of-town guests are already at the venue, which changes the energy of the night. People stay later, dance longer, and the evening has a natural momentum that hotel weddings with scattered accommodations don't always get.

Ceremony music is often on the terrace or outdoor grounds before guests move inside for cocktail hour and the Grand Ballroom reception. The hotel's full-service event team handles room logistics, so your entertainment vendor is focused purely on the sound and energy side.

Acoustic and logistics notes for Embassy Suites St. Augustine:

  • The Grand Ballroom is a standard hotel ballroom configuration — predictable acoustics, house power readily available, and enough floor space for a proper dance floor setup alongside dining tables.
  • Outdoor ceremony setups on the terrace should confirm power access in advance. The hotel's event coordinator can point your DJ to the closest outlet access or flag if a battery-powered system is the better call.
  • The St. Augustine location means guests often want to explore the historic district before or after the wedding. Build in a timeline that brings energy back into the room after dinner so the dance floor peaks at the right moment.

From our experience: The built-in hotel audience — guests who don't have to drive home — makes Embassy Suites receptions genuinely easier to keep going late. When the crowd knows they're already home for the night, the dance floor stays fuller longer.
