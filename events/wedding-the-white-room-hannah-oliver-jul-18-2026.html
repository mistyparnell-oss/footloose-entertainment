Hannah & Oliver — Wedding at The White Room

July 2026

Picture a quiet, polished floor under a chandelier, white fabric draped overhead and string lights glowing, and two people dancing alone while their guests watch from the tables.

That was how Hannah and Oliver's reception began at The White Room in St. Augustine. By the end of the night, that same floor was packed, and nobody was watching from the tables anymore. Footloose Entertainment was there as their wedding DJ and MC for the whole thing.

Oliver and Hannah took the floor in front of a room full of guests, the white drapes and string lights framing them. Hannah kissed him mid-dance while the room looked on.

As the MC, Footloose kept the evening moving so moments like this one got room to breathe. The room was quiet for the couple, and the music was ready for what came next.

It didn't take long. Guests of every age were out there, kids in yellow dresses included, all sharing one dance floor.

The lights turned purple, glow sticks came out, and the room switched from dinner mode to party mode. Guests danced in heart-shaped sunglasses, and one couple pulled off a full dip while friends cheered around them.

This is what a St. Augustine wedding DJ is there for: reading the room, then handing it back to the guests once they're ready.

The details were what made this crowd special. Guests waved paper fans printed with a dog's face, wore white heart-shaped sunglasses and held glow sticks high. One group squeezed in front of the photo booth in sunglasses for a shot.

It was a dance floor with personality, and the guests clearly had a great time. The DJ booth stayed right at the edge of it all, where every song and announcement could reach the whole room.
