Kailyn & Kevin — Wedding at The White Room

February 2026

Kailyn & Kevin celebrated their February 21, 2026 wedding at The White Room, bringing their family and friends together for a beautiful Florida wedding reception. DJ Dennis with Footloose Entertainment kept the wedding music going while Clare Sanborn captured the special moments throughout the day. Southern Palms Studio documented the celebration on video, while Tula Rose Floral Events added beautiful floral details to the wedding setting. From the carefully planned details to the energy on the dance floor, it was a wonderful celebration for Kailyn & Kevin and everyone who came together to celebrate with them. We were proud to be part of their wedding entertainment team and help keep the reception moving!
