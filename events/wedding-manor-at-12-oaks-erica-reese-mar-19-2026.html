Erica & Reese — Wedding at Manor at 12 Oaks

March 2026

Erica & Reese’s Wedding at The Manor at 12 Oaks in Green Cove Springs

On March 19, 2026, guests followed a brick aisle toward the pond at The Manor at 12 Oaks in Green Cove Springs, Florida. Wooden chairs faced the water, and a tall arch of pink and white flowers marked the spot where Erica and Reese would say “I do.”

It was a beautiful beginning to a celebration that would end with foam sticks in the air and a dance floor full of their favorite people. DJ Kris led the entertainment for Footloose Entertainment, providing wedding DJ and MC services, while our Open Air photo booth gave guests another way to capture memories throughout the celebration.

A LITTLE OF THEIR STORY BEFORE “I DO”

Among the wedding details was a sign that read “When We Were Two,” with childhood photos of Erica and Reese beside their names and wedding date. Before guests watched them begin married life, they got a small glimpse of where each of their stories started.

Outside, the pond and floral arch framed the ceremony as family and friends gathered to watch their next chapter begin.

FROM THE WATERFRONT TO THE DANCE FLOOR

As afternoon turned into evening, the courtyard became the gathering place. String lights hung above a black-and-white checkered dance floor, with our DJ setup positioned beside it, ready for introductions, toasts and the dancing ahead.

As Erica and Reese’s wedding DJ and MC, DJ Kris helped guide the reception through its planned moments while leaving room for the unexpected ones.

And there were plenty worth remembering.

A guest in a pink dress led a dog across the checkerboard. Friends brought their playful moves to the floor. Erica, wearing a denim jacket with “Mrs. Leggett” across the back, wrapped someone in a tight hug right in the middle of it all.

Alongside the dancing, Footloose Entertainment’s Open Air photo booth offered a place for friends and family to gather for photos—a little more of the celebration to take home.

THE PARTY PICKED UP AFTER DARK

By nighttime, guests packed the dance floor beneath the string lights. White foam sticks waved overhead as friends and family crowded around Erica and Reese, with DJ Kris keeping the music going.

Between the bigger party moments, the couple shared a slower dance together. Then it was back to celebrating with the people who had come to cheer them on.

That mix of excitement and quieter moments gave the evening its own rhythm—from the vows beside the pond to the last gathering on the dance floor.

ONE LAST WALK THROUGH THE GLOW

For the send-off, guests lined up with sparklers to see the newlyweds out. Erica and Reese walked through the glow together, bringing their wedding at The Manor at 12 Oaks to a beautiful close.

Congratulations, Erica and Reese! Thank you for choosing Footloose Entertainment to be part of your wedding day.
