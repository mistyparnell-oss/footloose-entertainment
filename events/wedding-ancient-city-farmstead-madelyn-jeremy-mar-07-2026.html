Wedding at Ancient City Farmstead — March 2026

March 2026

On March 7, 2026, Madelyn & Jeremy Conners celebrated their wedding with a beautiful ceremony at St. Anastasia Catholic Church followed by an unforgettable wedding reception at Ancient City Farmstead in St. Augustine, Florida. Every part of the day reflected their vision, creating a celebration that felt both timeless and uniquely their own.

Footloose Entertainment was honored to provide professional wedding DJ and entertainment services, with DJ Kris keeping the reception flowing effortlessly from the grand entrance and first dances to the final song of the evening. As guests filled the dance floor, every milestone of the reception was paired with the perfect soundtrack, creating memories that will be cherished for years to come.

The day was thoughtfully planned and designed by Kelsie Elizabeth Events, while Life & Love Studio beautifully documented every meaningful moment. Elegant floral arrangements by Flower Works SA, bridal beauty by Sarina Durden Beauty, stylish rentals from St. Johns Illuminations, delicious catering by MOJO BBQ, refreshing drinks from Perfect Pour Mobile Bar, and transportation by St. Aug Transportation all came together to create an exceptional wedding experience. From beginning to end, Madelyn & Jeremy's celebration showcased what happens when an incredible couple is surrounded by an outstanding team of wedding professionals.
